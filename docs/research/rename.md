# Choosing a new name (R27)

Research of 22 November 2026. Peter asked for a new name, avoiding Carryover, SideQuest and Tandem.

This is a first check against registries and web search, not a legal clearance.

## How it was checked

- **Decky:** checked against `SteamDeckHomebrew/decky-plugin-database`. The store site (plugins.deckbrew.xyz) is blocked by this laptop's network policy.
- **Registries:** PyPI, npm, AUR, Flathub and the Steam store, on the exact name.
- **GitHub:** a repository search.
- **Trademarks, phone apps, domains:** web search only. The UK IPO, USPTO and EUIPO search pages could not be reached directly.

## The top three

| Name | Clashes | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Haversack** | npm `haversack` (a small browser library, 2023); US trademark 6424354 for logistics software; an iOS truck-tracking app; a Swift keychain library. Free on PyPI, AUR, Flathub, Steam and Decky. | Clear in gaming; the uses are in freight and logistics |
| Packhorse | npm `packhorse` abandoned (2015); both US marks cancelled; a group-travel app; a UK flight-case maker | Clear in gaming |
| Wanderkit | Free on every registry; an iOS travel app owns wanderkit.org | Clear in software, but a coined word |

## Rejected

- **Crowded, a trademark in software, or a strong clash:** Tuckbox (weak fit), Hearthkit (Hearthstone), Twinpack (TwinCAT tool), Packmule, Pannier, Homebound (device-management software mark), Valise, Stowaway (a security tool), Coracle, Rucksack, Satchel, Kitbag.
- **Screened and dropped:** Ferryman, Wayfarer, Waystone, Cairn, Lodestar, Bellhop, Steward and Tagalong.
