# Haversack user guide

Haversack installs Windows games on your SteamOS and Bazzite handhelds, from an ISO, a folder or a network share, each into its own Steam shortcut and Proton prefix. It then sends them between your handhelds when you ask, directly over your home network, and keeps them in step after a patch or a mod. Everything happens in Gaming Mode with the controller.

## Where things are

Haversack was called Keepsake until version 0.51.0. Updating moved everything across, and nothing was lost ([Updates](updates.md)).

Version 0.61.0 took Haversack back to installing and moving games. Save sync, Advisor, the desktop app, PC support and the device pages are gone. Your saves in the cloud are still there: install version 0.60.0 to use them ([Updates](updates.md#going-back-to-a-version-with-save-sync)).

Haversack's page has four sections in its sidebar. Highlight one to see its pages, then press A to open one. On a section's page, **L1** and **R1** move between its pages, and **B** goes back to the sidebar. Each section opens where you left it.
- **Install.**
- **Games and devices:** Games, Moving games, Other devices, Emulators.
- **Settings:** General (with the quick menu's shortcuts), Modules, Updates, Help.
- **Diagnostics:** Device checks, Tools, Logs.

This guide is also in Haversack itself, under **Settings**, **Help**, so you can read it on the handheld.

The quick access menu shows games on their way between your devices, your chosen shortcuts, and anything that needs you.
